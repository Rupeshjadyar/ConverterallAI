How your typing score works

Five typed characters count as one standard word. For example, 250 correct characters in a 1-minute test gives you 50 net WPM.

Accuracy tracks your original attempts, so a typo recorded before Backspace still counts toward accuracy. Net WPM counts the correctly typed text remaining after corrections.
