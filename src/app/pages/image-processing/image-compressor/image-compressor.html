Common Questions & Help

How safe are my files here?

Your privacy is our priority. Since all compression runs directly inside your browser on your machine, your photos are never sent or stored on any server. Nobody else can access them.

Which image formats can I compress?

You can upload and compress JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, and BMP formats. You can also output your files as WEBP or PNG if you want to convert them on the fly.

How much can this tool reduce size?

Typically, size is reduced by 50% to 90% without any visible degradation in quality. Standard quality (80%) is ideal for most websites, emails, and social media uploads.
