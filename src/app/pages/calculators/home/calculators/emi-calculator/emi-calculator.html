What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount that a borrower pays to a lender every month until the loan is completely repaid. An EMI consists of both principal and interest components.

EMI calculators help borrowers estimate monthly repayments before applying for a loan, making financial planning easier and more accurate.

EMI Formula

The EMI calculation is based on the loan amount, annual interest rate, and repayment tenure.

Monthly EMI is calculated using a standard loan amortization formula.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment, which is a fixed monthly payment made towards loan repayment.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the loan amount, interest rate, and loan tenure.

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Yes. A longer loan tenure reduces monthly EMI but increases total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Can I use this calculator for home loans?

Yes. This EMI calculator can be used for home loans, personal loans, car loans, education loans, and most installment-based loans.
