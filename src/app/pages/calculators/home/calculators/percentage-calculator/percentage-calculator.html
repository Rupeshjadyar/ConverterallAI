About Percentage Calculator

A percentage calculator helps you calculate percentages, percentage increases, percentage decreases, discounts, profit margins, taxes, and growth rates quickly.

Whether you're a student, business owner, accountant, investor, or online shopper, percentage calculations are used daily.

Percentage Formula

  • Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
  • X% of Y = (X × Y) ÷ 100
  • Percentage Change = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100
  • Percentage Increase = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100
  • Percentage Decrease = ((Old − New) ÷ Old) × 100

Examples

  • 20% of 500 = 100
  • 25% of 400 = 100
  • 75 is what percent of 300 = 25%
  • 150 is what percent of 600 = 25%
  • 100 → 120 = 20% increase
  • 200 → 150 = 25% decrease

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate percentage?

Divide the part value by the whole value and multiply the result by 100.

What is 20% of 500?

20% of 500 equals 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the old value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100.
