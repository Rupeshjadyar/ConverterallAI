Learn Touch Typing Online Free – 12 Guided Masterclass Lessons

Touch typing is the method of typing without looking at the physical keyboard. By practicing the 12 structured modules in this course, you will systematically master the Home Row (ASDF JKL;), Top Row (QWERTY), Bottom Row (ZXCVBNM), and number/symbol keys with designated fingers.

1. Anchor on Home Row

Position your left index finger on F and right index finger on J. Always return your hands to these guide tactile bumps.

2. 98% Accuracy First

Typing fast with frequent typos wastes cognitive energy on backspacing. Prioritize hitting the correct key with the designated finger every time.

3. Daily 15-Min Habit

Just 15 minutes of focused touch typing per day builds permanent neuromuscular pathways, increasing speed from 30 to 80+ WPM within 4 weeks.
