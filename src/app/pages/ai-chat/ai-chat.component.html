Technical Overview & Architecture

Why Groq & Gemini Free Tiers Power ConverterAllAI

We combined the industry's most generous free API tiers with high-availability client-side failover orchestration. Developers get instant answers, code generation, and deep reasoning without unexpected rate-limit stops.

⚡

Groq LPU Hardware Acceleration

Groq uses Language Processing Units (LPUs) designed specifically for sequential tensor computation, clocking throughput exceeding 500 tokens/second. Free tier includes up to 14,400 requests per day and 30 requests per minute with near-zero latency.

✨

Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Reasoning

Gemini 1.5 Flash provides a groundbreaking 1-million token context window on Google AI Studio's free tier (1,500 requests per day). It handles large multi-file code reviews, deep logic, and architectural analysis effortlessly.

🔄

Seamless Auto-Failover Protocol

If your primary provider encounters an HTTP 429 (Rate Limit) or 503 (Overloaded) code, our Angular orchestration layer automatically captures the event, shifts down the priority chain (Groq ➔ Gemini ➔ OpenRouter ➔ HF), and completes your prompt without losing conversation state.

🔒

100% In-Browser Privacy

Your conversation history and custom API keys are processed strictly within your browser. No intermediary proxy inspects or stores your prompts. Requests dispatch directly to provider endpoints via native browser fetch.
